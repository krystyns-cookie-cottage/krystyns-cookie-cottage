Hey, I’m Krystyn

I’ve always shown my love through baking. Some of my favorite childhood memories were made in the kitchen with my grandma, my aunt, and my cousin — baking together, learning recipes, laughing, and making something sweet for the people we loved. Those moments stuck with me, and they’re a big part of why baking still means so much to me today.

Over the years, that love of baking grew into a love for decorating cookies, learning new techniques, and creating something special for other people’s celebrations. In 2026, that lifelong passion officially became Krystyn’s Cookie Cottage, a licensed cottage kitchen here in Chambersburg. What started as something I loved doing for family has grown into something I now get to share with all of you.

When I’m not covered in flour or royal icing, I’m a stepmom to two amazing young women and a very proud Lolly to my granddaughter, Kasper. I also work in operations for a trucking company — so yes, your cookie order will absolutely be organized. I’m a huge dog lover, an iced-coffee girl through and through, and most days you’ll find me at home with Mike when I’m not busy baking.

More than anything, I love being a small part of the moments that matter to you. Birthdays, showers, weddings, holidays, milestones, or just-because treats — there’s something really special about creating cookies that help make those memories even sweeter.
