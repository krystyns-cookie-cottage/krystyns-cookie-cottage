Hey, I'm Krystyn

I'm the baker, decorator, dishwasher, order-taker and social media department behind Krystyn's Cookie Cottage. The Cottage became a licensed cottage kitchen in Chambersburg in 2026, and I've been baking and decorating for years. I still fall more in love with it every day.

Off the cookie clock, I'm a stepmom to two amazing young women and a very proud Lolly to my granddaughter, Kasper. I work in operations for a trucking company (so yes, your order will be organized), I'm a huge dog lover, and you'll usually find me with an iced coffee in hand, at home with Mike.

Most of all, I love creating cookies that bring joy to your special moments.
